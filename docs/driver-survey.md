# BouRide Driver Survey - PMV & Taxi Operators

**Goal:** Get real data from AROB drivers before building backend.

## Questions for PMV Drivers - Arawa Market
1. What route do you drive most? Arawa-Buka? Buka-Buin?
2. How many trips per day? How many passengers per trip?
3. Current fare Arawa-Buka? Do you ever charge different prices?
4. Biggest problem: Empty seats? No passengers? Police? Road?
5. If passengers could pre-book via *777#, would you use it?
6. What commission is fair for BouRide? K1 per seat? K2?

## Questions for Taxi Operators - Buka Town/Airport
1. Where do you operate? Buka Town? Airport runs? Arawa?
2. Average fare: Buka Market to Bel Isi? Buka to Airport?
3. How do passengers find you now? Phone calls? Wait at stand?
4. Biggest problem: No customers? Fuel cost? Other taxis?
5. If BouRide sent you passenger requests via SMS, would you use it?
6. What commission per trip is fair? K2? K3?

## For All Drivers
7. Phone type: 4G smartphone or 2G button phone?
8. How do you want to get paid? Mobile money? Weekly cash?
9. Can we call you for pilot testing? Get phone number.

## Field Notes
- Interview 5 PMV drivers at Arawa Market 8am-10am
- Interview 3 taxi operators at Buka Market/Airport 11am-1pm
- Buy them K5 buai. Respect their time.
- Take photo of plate with permission
- Record voice notes if they agree
